My approach

A continuous cycle of evidence and innovation

Much of what health research proves never reaches the patients and communities it could serve. I translate established evidence into scalable products, services, and strategies.

Each innovation, especially AI-enabled solutions, then becomes a source of new data. That data generates evidence that strengthens the next decision. Throughout the cycle, I weigh financial sustainability against the human values that health care depends on.

"Business with humanity in mind."

Evidence-based

Clinical training grounds my work in established research, so solutions start from what is proven to improve outcomes.

Financially sustainable

Health solutions need viable business models to scale. I design for revenue as well as impact, so that the impact lasts.

Equitable by design

AI → new evidence

Speak Safe dashboard: turning silence into evidence

An employer-facing dashboard I designed for Speak Safe. Anonymous AI check-ins are coded into themes, then shown only at group level, so leaders can see what gets in the way without seeing anyone's answers.

  • Privacy threshold. Groups below a minimum size are pooled, never dropped, so no one is identifiable or erased.
  • AI theme analysis. Language analysis surfaces barriers such as fear of disclosure, stigma, and inflexible policy.
  • Policy simulator. Estimates productive days recovered under a new policy, starting from published survey data.

Speak Safe: making menstrual burden at work visible

Most people hide menstrual symptoms at work, so the burden never reaches HR or public health data. Speak Safe uses anonymous AI check-ins to turn that silence into group-level evidence for workplace and health policy, without exposing any individual.
